Privacy Policy

App: TV Radio  |  Last updated: September 5, 2026

GGG Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the TV Radio application for Android TV (the "App"). This page explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

In short: TV Radio does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDK of any kind.

Information We Collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data about you. The App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Favorites and recently played — the stations you save and listen to
  • Region setting — the country you select for local stations
  • Display preferences — such as whether the ambient background is enabled

Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

Third-Party Services the App Connects To

Radio-Browser

The App uses Radio-Browser, a community-maintained open database, to obtain station listings, search and categories. Requests to that service necessarily include your IP address (as with any network request) and a User-Agent string identifying the App.

When you start playing a station, the App reports a single anonymous play count to that service, following its documented conventions — this is how station popularity rankings are maintained. The report contains no personally identifiable information.

Station Stream Servers

During playback the App connects directly to the stream URL of the station you selected. These servers are operated by the individual broadcasters or their providers, are outside our control, and will see your IP address. Each broadcaster may have its own privacy policy.

Station Artwork

Station logos are loaded from URLs supplied by the stations themselves. Those hosts will likewise see your IP address.

Permissions

  • Internet & network state — required to fetch station listings and play streams
  • Foreground service & wake lock — keeps audio playing after you leave the player screen or turn the display off
  • Notifications — shows the media notification so the system and your remote can control playback

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Content

TV Radio is a player. It neither produces nor hosts any audio content — all streams are provided and operated by third-party broadcasters. If you are a broadcaster and do not wish your stream to appear in the App, please contact us at the address below, or request removal directly from the Radio-Browser database.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
